Until now, updates for FamilyManager arrived irregularly. Sometimes nothing for weeks, then one big drop, most recently version 3.0 on 1 November with the new look and the structure aids. I am changing that. From version 3.0.2, FamilyManager ships **every Wednesday** with an update.

I am a solo developer with a full-time job on the side. Claude helps me build, test and plan. That is exactly why I need a fixed rhythm that holds up in busy weeks too.

## What to expect every Wednesday

Mostly small point versions: bugs that are gone, and polish. Now and then a bigger version comes along. The next one is 3.1, the "everyday update", planned for December.

## How a week works

- **Monday morning is the cut-off.** Whatever is finished by then and has run with the testers goes out. Whatever is not takes the next Wednesday.
- **Tuesday** is polish, and I submit the update to Apple.
- **Wednesday** I release it in the App Store. The web version gets it the same day.

## Testers get builds earlier

Independent of Wednesday, TestFlight runs continuously. As soon as something is finished, a test build goes straight to the testers, sometimes several times a day. That gives them more than a day or two to try it out and give me feedback.

## The rules

- **No empty release just for the date.** If nothing is finished, there is no update on Wednesday.
- **An item that is not finished does not hold the release back.** It ships a week later.
- **Bigger features stay switched off until they are finished.** That way I can work with them early without them reaching you half-done.
- **I pause over Christmas,** roughly 22 to 28 December, because Apple reviews more slowly then.

## Why I am doing this

Small, predictable steps have three advantages for me: bugs are gone sooner, each update carries less risk, and feedback reaches the app faster. A big drop after weeks of silence is harder for everyone involved to judge.
