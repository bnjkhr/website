From now on, news about my products will appear right here on my website. Not just finished releases, but also decisions, detours and things I learn while building.

The focus is on FamilyManager, Track4Kids, GymBo and the other products I'm working on. The blog is the permanent home for these posts. If you don't want to miss anything, you can get notified by email or subscribe to the RSS feed.

The existing newsletter is moving over from Substack step by step. Older posts will follow once the content, images and links have been carried over cleanly.
