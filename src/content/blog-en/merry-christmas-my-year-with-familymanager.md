Today is Christmas Eve, and I want to thank you. For using FamilyManager, trying it out or simply reading along here. I wish you and your family a peaceful Christmas and a few quiet days, with no schedule at all.

2026 was an exciting year for me. Here is a quick look back at what happened.

## The year in bullet points

- **February:** The first lines of code. The idea was simple: one app where a family has tasks, events, shopping and documents in one place, without five different apps and without a constant chat.
- **Summer:** Version 2 and, on 16 July, version 2.1 in the App Store. Updates followed in quick succession up to the 2.2.x versions in late summer.
- **July:** I found out that many of my emails never reached some of the users, and fixed it. I will write about that in more detail in December.
- **Autumn:** A new navigation, a search for everything, the custody schedule right in the calendar, plus many small things you notice in everyday use.
- **1 November:** Version 3.0 is in the App Store. With a new look that feels like an Apple app on the iPhone, and with the structure aids for people who find planning hard.
- **November:** From now on there is an update every Wednesday, and in December the advent calendar opened door by door.

## What I learned

- **Leaving things out is work.** Before 3.0 I removed more than I added. The app did not get smaller, but it got clearer.
- **Feedback beats my assumptions.** Much of what I thought was important was not. Much of what I thought was minor made someone's day easier.
- **A fixed rhythm beats one big drop.** That is why Wednesday.
- **Measure instead of guess.** Most of my mistakes I did not see, I measured them.

## Thank you

More than a thousand people use FamilyManager now. I would not have expected that in February, and it still feels a little unreal every time I read it.

My thanks go to everyone who wrote to me, even when the message started with "This does not work". To those who tested new versions early and told me exactly what was wrong. And to everyone who recommended FamilyManager, to a sister, a neighbour or the parents from daycare.

I build FamilyManager on my own, next to my day job. Every piece of feedback helps me build the right things first.

## What comes next

I am taking a break over Christmas and New Year. The last update before the break came on 16 December, the next one comes on **13 January**. After that the Wednesday rhythm continues.

Until then: close your task list, leave the calendar alone and enjoy the days.

Merry Christmas, and a good start to the new year!

Ben
